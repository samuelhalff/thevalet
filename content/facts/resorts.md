## Geneva Airport (GVA)

- access | Geneva Airport (GVA) is on the edge of the city of Geneva, at Cointrin.
- access | The airport has its own railway station, linked to Geneva-Cornavin and the Swiss rail network.
- access | The airport has a Swiss sector and a French sector.
- access | Private and business aviation is handled at Geneva Airport.
- access | Geneva Airport is the usual international gateway for the Swiss and French Alpine resorts listed below.

## Verbier

- access | Verbier is in the canton of Valais, above Le Châble in the Val de Bagnes.
- access | By road from Geneva: along Lake Geneva, up the Rhône valley to Martigny, then into the Val de Bagnes.
- access | By rail: trains run via Martigny to Le Châble, where a gondola goes up to Verbier.
- access | Sion has an airport used by private aviation, in the Rhône valley.
- stay | Verbier is part of the 4 Vallées ski area.
- stay | Verbier is a winter resort and also a summer resort (hiking, mountain biking).
- stay | Accommodation is largely in chalets and apartments, with a lively village centre.
- event | Verbier Festival: classical music festival, every summer.
- event | Xtreme Verbier: freeride competition on the Bec des Rosses, at the end of winter.

## Zermatt

- access | Zermatt is in the canton of Valais, at the head of the Mattertal, reached from Visp.
- access | Zermatt is car-free: private cars and chauffeured vehicles stop at Täsch.
- access | From Täsch, a shuttle train runs to Zermatt; the Visp–Zermatt railway also serves the village.
- access | Inside Zermatt, electric taxis and hotel electric vehicles carry passengers and luggage.
- access | Zermatt has a heliport.
- stay | Zermatt lies at the foot of the Matterhorn.
- stay | The ski area links across the border with Breuil-Cervinia in Italy.
- stay | Zermatt is open in winter and summer, with high-altitude skiing and mountaineering.
- event | Zermatt Unplugged: music festival, every spring.

## Gstaad

- access | Gstaad is in the Saanenland, in the canton of Bern (Bernese Oberland), close to the Vaud border.
- access | By road from Geneva: via the Lake Geneva region, then through the Pays-d'Enhaut.
- access | By rail: the line from Montreux through the Pays-d'Enhaut serves Gstaad.
- access | Saanen, next to Gstaad, has an airfield used by small aircraft and helicopters.
- stay | Gstaad's village centre is built around a largely pedestrian promenade.
- stay | The resort is known for chalets, a quiet village atmosphere and both winter and summer seasons.
- event | Menuhin Festival Gstaad: classical music festival, every summer.
- event | Swiss Open Gstaad: tennis tournament, every summer.

## St-Moritz

- access | St-Moritz is in the Engadin, in the canton of Graubünden, far to the east of Geneva.
- access | From Geneva the journey is long; travellers often go via Zurich or fly.
- access | Engadin Airport at Samedan, near St-Moritz, handles private aviation.
- access | By road, the Engadin is reached over Alpine passes such as the Julier, or with the Vereina car-carrying train.
- access | By rail: the Rhaetian Railway serves St-Moritz; the Glacier Express runs between Zermatt and St-Moritz.
- stay | St-Moritz hosted the Winter Olympics twice (1928 and 1948).
- stay | Winter events take place on the frozen lake of St-Moritz.
- stay | The Cresta Run and the natural-ice bobsleigh run are part of the resort's winter tradition.
- event | Snow Polo World Cup St. Moritz: polo on the frozen lake, every winter.
- event | White Turf: horse racing on the frozen lake, every winter.

## Courchevel

- access | Courchevel is in Savoie, France, above the Tarentaise valley.
- access | By road from Geneva: towards Albertville, then up the Tarentaise valley via Moûtiers.
- access | The nearest railway station is Moûtiers.
- access | Courchevel has an altiport, used by small aircraft and helicopters.
- stay | Courchevel is part of the Trois Vallées ski area.
- stay | The resort is made up of several villages at different levels, including Courchevel 1850, Moriond and Le Praz.
- stay | Courchevel is mainly a winter resort, with chalets, apartments and hotels.

## Megève

- access | Megève is in Haute-Savoie, France, in the Val d'Arly.
- access | By road from Geneva: along the Arve valley towards Sallanches, then up to Megève.
- access | The nearest railway station is Sallanches-Combloux-Megève.
- access | Megève has an altiport, used by small aircraft.
- stay | Megève is a historic village resort with a pedestrian medieval centre.
- stay | Megève is part of the Évasion Mont-Blanc ski area.
- stay | Megève is open in winter and summer.

## Chamonix

- access | Chamonix-Mont-Blanc is in Haute-Savoie, France, at the foot of Mont Blanc.
- access | By road from Geneva: the Autoroute Blanche (A40) along the Arve valley, then up to Chamonix.
- access | The Mont Blanc Tunnel links Chamonix with Courmayeur in Italy.
- access | By rail: the Mont-Blanc Express runs from Martigny (Switzerland); the French line comes from Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet.
- stay | Chamonix hosted the first Winter Olympics (1924).
- stay | The Aiguille du Midi cable car and the Montenvers train to the Mer de Glace start from the valley.
- stay | The Vallée Blanche is an off-piste descent, done with a mountain guide.
- stay | Chamonix is a mountaineering centre, open in winter and summer.
- event | UTMB (Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc): trail race around Mont Blanc, every late summer.
