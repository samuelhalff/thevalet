## Private concierge

### What you can hand over to us

A private concierge exists to make happen what you do not have time to organise yourself. In Geneva, our requests range from the very simple (a table, a ticket, a gift delivered to the right address) to the ongoing (running a household, a multi-stop journey).

#### Personal assistance

- Sourcing and buying gifts for a specific occasion, with a budget and delivery date set in advance.
- Bespoke research: a particular experience, a craftsman, an expert in a given field.
- Everyday practical and administrative errands, using the documents you send us.
- Keeping a statement of expenses incurred on your behalf, request by request.
- Handling unexpected requests and reorganising what needs to change when your plans move.

#### Daily life and the household

We oversee the running of your home (cleaning, laundry, gardening), welcoming visitors, keys and the follow-up of repairs. When that becomes regular, it falls under our [residence management in Geneva and Vaud](/en/services/residence-management/). We also coordinate getting around, with a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) for an evening or a day of meetings.

#### Reservations, travel and hotels

Restaurants, performances, tickets, hotels and residences: we book according to your dates and preferences, in Switzerland and abroad. For a mountain stay, our page on [chalets and residences in the Alps](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/) sets out what we arrange resort by resort.

### Hotels and residences worldwide

Booking a hotel is often the simplest part of a trip, yet the one where details matter: a connecting room for the children, a quiet floor, a late arrival announced, a transfer timed to the flight. We look for the property that fits your plans and budget, a city hotel or somewhere more secluded, then pass on your preferences before you arrive.

For longer or family stays, we also look for private residences and holiday rentals: a villa, a city apartment or a chalet. Each proposal states what is included, the cancellation terms and the services we can add on site, such as a private chef or housekeeping during the stay.

### What helps us to know

A clear request is handled faster. Depending on the case, tell us:

- the aim and the deadline: by when, for whom, for what occasion;
- the budget, or at least a range, and what matters most (timing, price, the character of the place);
- your known preferences: dietary needs, tastes, brands or styles to avoid;
- who to contact, and who not to copy in;
- how you want to be kept informed: a message at each step or a single report.

If you do not yet know what you want, just say so: part of our work is to offer two or three reasoned options.

### Lead times and availability

Requests received 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; lead times and availability confirmed for each request. What affects timing is rarely our own hours and mostly other people's: a fully booked restaurant, a shop closed on Sundays, a craftsman who works to order, a busy period in Geneva. When a request cannot be met in the time you want, we tell you and offer an alternative rather than wait.

### What we do ourselves, and what we entrust

We do the research, comparison, booking and follow-up ourselves. The services themselves are delivered by third parties: restaurants, hotels, craftsmen, couriers, housekeeping companies. For regulated services (passenger transport, security, health, legal or property advice) we work with independent partners, chosen for their authorised activity; The Valet introduces and coordinates, but does not provide these services itself. That is the case for our [medical concierge](/en/services/medical-concierge/) and for [planning a wedding or private event](/en/services/weddings-private-events/), where we coordinate several providers around a single date.

Before any booking, we confirm in writing the provider, what is included and on what terms.

### The questions we will ask you

For a first request, we will ask how and when to reach you, whether anyone else may instruct us on your behalf (an assistant, a family member), and how you prefer to settle expenses incurred for you. For ongoing support, we will also agree what we may decide on our own and what must be put to you first.

You can [send a request to our concierge](/en/request/?service=concierge) in a few lines: we come back with precise questions or a first proposal.

## Medical concierge

The Valet's medical concierge handles the logistics around your healthcare appointments in Geneva and Switzerland: booking appointments with independent healthcare professionals, transport, nearby accommodation and the practical organisation of consultations and tests. The Valet does not provide medical advice or care: diagnosis, treatment and every medical decision rest solely with the healthcare professionals you consult.

A medical appointment abroad, or simply in an unfamiliar city, involves a great deal of organisation that has nothing medical about it: finding a slot, working out where the practice is, arranging the journey, booking a nearby hotel, fitting a test and a consultation into the same day, planning the return. That is the part we take on, so you can focus on the appointment itself.

### Our role, and its limits

The Valet does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or care, and does not assess whether a treatment is appropriate. We do not employ healthcare professionals. Consultations, tests and treatment are provided by independent professionals and facilities, who set their own terms, fees and waiting times.

Our work is logistical: organising, booking, coordinating and practical support. We make no medical decision on your behalf and do not act as a go-between for health information.

### What we can organise

- **Appointments.** Booking a consultation, check-up or test according to the specialty and city you name, and grouping several appointments into one period where possible.
- **Journeys.** Transfers from the airport or station, trips to the practice or clinic and back, with a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) through our partner operators. Medical transport is outside this scope: it is a matter for specialised services.
- **Accommodation.** A nearby hotel or apartment, with what you need for a recovery stay (access, quiet, flexible length).
- **The stay.** Restaurant bookings, arrangements for a companion or family, and any practical request handled by our [private concierge in Geneva](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/) during your stay.
- **After the appointment.** Arranging a follow-up if the professional asks for one, extending accommodation, changing the return journey.

### Your health information

Please do not send medical information to The Valet through the website form, by message or by email. To organise an appointment we need to know which specialty or type of test you are looking for, in which city, on which dates, and your practical constraints. The reason for the consultation, medical history, test results and prescriptions are shared directly with the healthcare professional, through the channels they indicate.

This rule protects your privacy and keeps data away from a non-medical intermediary who has no need for it.

### How a request works

1. **First exchange.** You tell us the specialty or type of test, the city, the period and your travel constraints. You can [ask us to organise medical appointments](/en/request/?service=concierge) with these details alone.
2. **Proposals.** Depending on the specialty, we suggest independent professionals or facilities and the availability we have been able to obtain. The choice is yours.
3. **Bookings.** Once the appointment is set, we arrange transport and accommodation around it and send you a written summary of times, addresses and contacts.
4. **During the stay.** We remain reachable for practical adjustments: a moved appointment, an earlier pick-up, an extra night.

### What affects lead times

Lead times depend first on the healthcare professionals: some specialties have full diaries, and some tests require a prescription or preparation that only the professional can specify. The period also matters for accommodation, particularly during the large trade fairs and conferences that fill Geneva's hotels. We tell you what is possible once availability has been checked, without promising a slot we do not control.

### The questions we will ask you

- The specialty or type of test, with no detail about your health.
- The preferred city or region, and whether you already have a professional in mind.
- Your available dates and where you are travelling from.
- The number of companions and their needs.
- Your practical travel needs: mobility, luggage, wheelchair, step-free access.
- The language you would like to be seen in, which we mention when booking.

In a medical emergency, do not go through The Valet: call 144 or 112. If you live in Geneva and are looking instead for regular help at home, our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) service can also organise the practical side of the household during a period of convalescence.

### Medical concierge questions

#### Does The Valet give medical advice?

No. The Valet does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or care. We organise the logistics; the independent healthcare professionals you consult are solely responsible for advice and treatment.

#### What can you organise?

Appointment booking, transport there and back, nearby accommodation, coordinating times across several appointments and, on request, practical support during your stay.

#### Do I need to send you medical information?

No. Please do not include health details in the website form. We only need the specialty, the city, the dates and your practical preferences; medical information is shared directly with the healthcare professional.

#### Can you choose a doctor for me?

We do not recommend treatment or judge the merits of medical advice. Depending on the specialty and city, we can suggest independent professionals and book with the one you choose.

#### Do you handle emergencies?

No. In a medical emergency, call 144, the Swiss emergency medical number, or 112 immediately.

#### How are medical fees paid?

Medical fees are invoiced directly by the healthcare professionals or facilities. Our coordination service is quoted and invoiced separately.
