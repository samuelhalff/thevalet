A property project takes time before the first viewing. You need to find the right contacts, describe what you are looking for, organise travel and bring different diaries together. Whether you plan to buy, sell or rent in Switzerland, The Valet can introduce independent professionals and handle the practical organisation around those conversations. You retain the decision about the property, the terms offered and the professionals with whom you wish to work.

## A contact for practical coordination

We start by understanding your request: the nature of the project, the area concerned, your availability and the practical steps with which you would like help. We then introduce professionals suited to the need you describe. They present their services, examine what is possible and confirm their engagement directly with you.

The Valet is not an estate agency, law firm or notary. We do not carry out valuations or advise on the value of a property or whether a transaction is suitable for you. Our involvement covers introductions and coordination. Each professional remains responsible for their work and for the information they give you. You decide whether to take the conversation further after the introduction.

## Buying, selling or renting

For a purchase, we can facilitate first contact with property professionals and organise the viewings you select. For a sale, we can introduce an independent professional who will explain their approach and conditions. For a rental, coordination may cover appointments, travel and practical steps before your arrival.

In each case, tell us from the start what you expect from our involvement. A single request, such as organising one day of viewings, involves different arrangements from a move with several appointments. We agree what needs coordinating before starting. Property availability and acceptance of an application do not depend on The Valet. Any offer or proposal should be discussed directly with the relevant professional before you decide how to proceed.

## Viewings and travel

When you come to visit, we can bring appointments into a schedule, confirm addresses and arrange journeys with a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/). Where several people are involved, we coordinate times according to their availability and your instructions. Accommodation during a search trip can also form part of the arrangements requested.

For a seasonal rental or mountain stay, see our [Alpine chalets and residences](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/) service. That page covers stays and their organisation, while this page concerns introductions around a property project. Sharing the purpose of your visit helps us direct your request to the appropriate service and avoid unnecessary arrangements.

## Legal questions have their own service

A transaction may raise questions about contracts, financing, tax or co-ownership. Buyers resident abroad may also have questions about the conditions for acquisition. These matters are examined by the relevant professionals; we do not decide them. Our separate [legal](/en/services/legal-real-estate/) service explains introductions to independent lawyers. They confirm whether they can act and on what terms, directly with you.

## Once the property is chosen

We can prepare practical moving arrangements: coordinating removals, opening the house and arranging appointments. Our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) service can then provide regular care according to the needs agreed. Our [private concierge](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/) service can also handle everyday requests around your arrival. You can discuss these needs separately from the property transaction so that the responsibilities of each person involved remain clear.

## Requests and terms

To [request a property introduction](/en/request/?service=real-estate), simply describe your project, the area, your availability and the practical help you need. Do not send sensitive documents through the form. The professional presents their own fees directly; our coordination is covered by a separate proposal where applicable. This gives you an opportunity to understand who will act and what their work covers before you make a commitment. Any change to the practical arrangements can then be discussed with your contact at The Valet.
