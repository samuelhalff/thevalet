A legal question rarely comes at a convenient time: a contract to review before signing, a dispute with a supplier, a move to Switzerland to prepare. The first difficulty is often knowing whom to ask. That is where we come in: pointing you to the right independent professional, then organising what surrounds the matter.

## An introduction, not advice

The Valet does not provide legal advice, draft legal documents or represent anyone. We are not a law firm, an estate agency or a notary. Our role is to understand the nature of your need, suggest one or more relevant independent professionals and arrange the first contact.

From there, the relationship is directly between you and the professional. They confirm whether they can act, within what scope, on what terms and for what fees. Professional privilege and responsibility for the advice are theirs.

## Areas covered by our partner lawyers

Our partner lawyers can provide:

- legal consultations, one-off or ongoing;
- drafting and reviewing contracts;
- negotiations on their client's behalf;
- dispute handling and representation;
- legal research on a specific question.

They work in particular in business, family, criminal and immigration law. For a highly specialised question, we look for the right professional rather than sending you to a generalist.

## Preparing the first contact

A few details are enough to point you in the right direction:

- the area of the question (contract, family, business, immigration, criminal, property);
- the canton or city concerned, and the country if the matter is international;
- the language you would like to work in;
- the timetable: a deadline, a hearing, a signing date;
- whether you prefer a first conversation by phone, video call or in person.

Please do not attach documents or describe the substance of the matter in the website form. You can [request an introduction to a lawyer](/en/request/?service=legal-real-estate) in a few lines; documents then go directly to the professional chosen.

## Lead times, fees and engagement

A lawyer's availability depends on their diary, on the conflict-of-interest checks they must carry out before accepting a matter, and on the complexity of the question. If the matter is urgent, say so from the start: we take it into account when choosing whom to contact, without being able to promise an appointment on a given date.

Fees are set and invoiced by the professional, who presents them before the engagement begins. If our involvement goes beyond the introduction (viewings, travel, moving in), it is the subject of a separate proposal.

## When the matter touches other needs

Settling in Geneva often combines several tasks: finding a home, enrolling children in school, organising transport, running the household. Our [private concierge in Geneva](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/) can take care of these practical aspects in parallel, while legal questions stay between you and your lawyer.

## Practical arrangements around your appointment

A first conversation with a lawyer may involve travel, time in a busy diary or coordination between several people. Our role is to make these arrangements easier, following your instructions. We can arrange travel with a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) and bring together practical appointment details: location, time, preferred language and contact information.

You remain free to choose your lawyer and decide whether to continue after the first meeting. An introduction does not mean the professional has accepted your matter and does not constitute a consultation. If you have a specific deadline, tell us so that we can pass it to the professional approached. Only they can confirm availability and the steps they can take.

For a property search, viewings or moving in, see our separate [real estate](/en/services/real-estate/) service. It covers introductions to property professionals and coordination of viewings. Questions of property law remain with independent lawyers.

For arrangements at home during appointments or after moving in, our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) service can help with daily practical needs. These arrangements are distinct from the legal engagement and are defined with you according to the needs you describe.
