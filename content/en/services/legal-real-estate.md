## What we arrange

Some requests go beyond concierge work and call for a regulated professional. Our role is to find the right person, make the first contact easy and handle the logistics around the matter.

### Legal services through partner lawyers

Depending on each firm's expertise, partner lawyers offer:

- legal consultations;
- contract drafting and review;
- negotiation;
- dispute management;
- representation, where appropriate;
- legal research.

They work in particular in business, family, criminal and immigration law.

### Real estate support

Property and construction call for precise expertise, given the complexity of transactions, regulations and financial stakes. Our partners, lawyers and property professionals, can handle requests relating to property transactions, rentals and construction matters across Switzerland.

## How an introduction works

1. **Your request.** You describe the area involved and the kind of help you need, in general terms: for example "review of a lease", "a family law question", "finding a flat to rent in Geneva". The details of the matter are not needed at this stage.
2. **Identification.** We identify one or more independent professionals whose expertise matches your request.
3. **First contact.** We arrange the initial conversation. The professional confirms the scope, fees and terms directly with you.
4. **Coordination.** If you wish, we handle the logistics: appointments, passing on documents, travel, translations arranged through providers.

## Typical situations

- A family moving to Switzerland who need both a lawyer for immigration questions and a professional to find a home to rent.
- A contract to review before signing, in a language or legal system you are less familiar with.
- Buying a chalet or a flat, where a lawyer, a property professional and viewings need to be coordinated.
- A dispute with a supplier or a neighbour, on which you would like an initial view from a lawyer.
- An owner living elsewhere who needs to manage the rental of their property remotely.

## Where

**Geneva and Vaud.** Introductions are most frequent here, for legal questions as well as rentals and transactions around Lake Geneva.

**Valais, Bern and Graubünden.** Property questions in the resorts (purchase, seasonal rental, building work) are often governed by specific cantonal and municipal rules; the professional you appoint knows and confirms them. For a stay without buying, see our [Alpine chalets and residences](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/).

**The French Alps.** For a property in France, French law applies; the introduction is then made, case by case, to a professional working within that framework.

## Who does what

The Valet does not provide legal advice. We identify professionals, make the introduction and coordinate communication and logistics.

Regulated legal and property services are supplied independently by appropriately licensed lawyers and property professionals. Each one confirms the scope of their work, their fees and the applicable regulatory conditions directly with you, and remains responsible for their advice.

## How we choose the right person

A good introduction takes several criteria into account: the area of law or type of property, the canton concerned, the language you want to work in, and the urgency and complexity of the question. We explain why we are suggesting someone; if the first conversation does not suit you, we look for another professional.

## After signing

Once the lease or deed is signed, everyday life begins: handing over keys, upkeep, following up on works. Our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) can take care of this, and our [private concierge in Geneva](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/) handles the practical steps of settling in.

## Confidentiality

We pass on to the professional only what is needed for the introduction. The details of your matter are then shared directly with them. To begin, you can [request an introduction to a lawyer or property professional](/en/request/?service=legal-real-estate), describing your need in a few words.
