Getting ready at home changes the rhythm of an important day. No trip to a salon, no waiting, and preparation happens somewhere familiar, at the time that suits the rest of the programme. That is the idea behind the Glam Team: bringing the beauty professions you need to your appointment, and having them arrive together, at the right moment.

## For which occasions

- **A wedding.** The bride, but also bridesmaids, mothers, witnesses, sometimes the groom. Preparation usually takes place at the hotel or the family home, with touch-ups possible before the ceremony, between photos and before the evening.
- **A private party or gala.** For you, or for several hosts getting ready in one place before leaving together.
- **A photo shoot.** Portrait, family photography, catalogue: makeup designed for the camera and the light on location.
- **A public appearance.** Conference, interview, award ceremony, filming: quick, clean preparation that fits your schedule.
- **A stay in Geneva.** For guests at a hotel who do not know the city's professionals.

## Who is on the team

Depending on the request, the team brings together one or more independent professionals:

- **Makeup.** Applied with professional products, enhancing natural features and lasting from the ceremony to the end of the evening.
- **Hair.** Chignons, waves, classic or more contemporary styles, built to hold in context: wind outdoors, the warmth of a room, a veil or accessory to fasten.
- **Styling.** Advice on the outfit according to silhouette, colouring and occasion, and the balance between outfit, hair and makeup.
- **Additional care.** Skin preparation the evening before or that morning, manicure and pedicure on request.

We choose the professionals according to the look you want and the occasion. If you have seen work you like, send us one or two images: it is often the quickest way to understand each other.

## The trial and the wedding-day schedule

For a wedding, we recommend a trial a few weeks before the date. It settles the makeup and hair, tests how a style holds with the veil or an accessory, and above all measures the time each person really needs.

From there we build a preparation schedule working back from departure time: who goes first, how many professionals are needed for everyone to be ready on time, when the photographer arrives. The schedule is shared with everyone involved and, if the event is organised with us, built into the running order of our [weddings and private events](/en/services/weddings-private-events/) service.

## What we need from you

- the occasion, the date and the time you need to be ready;
- the address for preparation (home, hotel, venue) and access details;
- the number of people and, for each, the services wanted;
- the look you have in mind, with reference images if you have them;
- the outfit, or at least its colour and neckline;
- any skin sensitivity or product to avoid, without medical detail.

With these details you can [book the Glam Team for your preparation](/en/request/?service=glam-team); we come back with a proposed team and timings.

## Practical constraints

Light matters: a bright room near a window gives a truer result than a dark bathroom. Plan a quiet space, a table, a chair and a power socket for each professional. At a hotel, check that the room or suite can take several people at once.

The busiest dates, especially Saturdays in wedding season and evenings of major events in Geneva, book earlier. Outside Geneva, the team's travel is arranged and priced separately; for preparation in the mountains, we coordinate with the arrangements for your stay described on our [Alpine chalets and residences](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/) page.

## What the service includes

The proposal lists the professionals planned, their arrival and departure times, the services per person, any touch-ups during the event and travel costs. Products and equipment are brought by the team. If a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) is then taking you to the ceremony or the evening, we time the car to the end of preparation.

We ask the professionals to respect the privacy of your appointment and your home, and not to take photographs or post anything without your express consent.
