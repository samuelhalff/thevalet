## What we arrange

The Glam Team brings the beauty professions together around your appointment, so that you have one contact and one schedule.

- **Makeup.** Applied with premium products and professional techniques, it enhances your features and takes the light into account: daylight, evening, flash or studio.
- **Hair.** Classic or contemporary styles, chignons, waves, bridal hair. Each style is designed to last through the event and suit outdoor conditions: wind off the lake, humidity, mountain cold.
- **Wardrobe styling.** Advice according to your figure, colouring and taste; choosing the outfit and accessories and bringing them together.
- **Additional care.** Skin preparation, manicure and pedicure on request.
- **Touch-ups.** Someone on hand during the event or between key moments, if you wish.

## How a booking works

1. **A first conversation.** You tell us the occasion, the date, the time you need to be ready, the place and the number of people. Photos of your outfit and inspiration are welcome.
2. **The consultation.** Makeup, hair and style preferences are discussed with the professionals, for a result that is consistent with you and with the occasion.
3. **The team and the schedule.** We put together the right team and set a preparation timetable, then confirm the terms with you. For a wedding, a trial can be arranged beforehand.
4. **On the day.** The professionals arrive at the agreed time with their equipment, set up in the space provided and work at your pace.
5. **During the event.** Touch-ups can be arranged, particularly between the ceremony and the evening.

## Typical situations

- A bride getting ready at her hotel with her mother and bridesmaids, who needs to be ready for photos before leaving for the ceremony.
- A gala evening for which you want hair and makeup done at home in the late afternoon, without going to a salon.
- A photo shoot or filming where makeup has to hold under lights and be adjusted between takes.
- A public appearance or a conference, with quick, discreet preparation in a dressing room or hotel room.
- A mountain stay during which an important evening is prepared at the chalet rather than in town.

## Where the Glam Team works

**In Geneva.** At home, at your hotel or at the reception venue. This is the most common setting: you stay at home or in your room, and the team comes to you.

**Vaud and the Lake Geneva region.** The same services at home, in hotels or at the estates where weddings and receptions take place.

**Valais, Gstaad and St-Moritz.** In the resorts, preparation often takes place at the chalet or hotel. The professionals' travel, sometimes over several days, is arranged in advance; in [Gstaad](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/#gstaad) or [Verbier](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/#verbier), we coordinate their visit with the rest of your stay.

**The French Alps.** Megève, Courchevel or Chamonix are possible depending on availability, on the same basis: place, timings and terms confirmed for each request.

## Who does what

The Valet takes your request, puts the team together, sets the schedule, confirms the terms and remains your contact before and during the appointment. If your plans change, you tell us.

Makeup, hair, styling and care are carried out by independent beauty professionals, with their own equipment and products. If you have an allergy, sensitive skin or products you prefer to avoid, tell the professionals directly during the consultation.

## Bridal hair and makeup in Geneva

On the wedding day, the time spent getting ready matters as much as the result. We arrange a trial beforehand if you wish, then a precise schedule on the day for the bride, those close to her and, if needed, the groom. If we are also planning your reception, the Glam Team fits into the running order of your [wedding and private event](/en/services/weddings-private-events/); for the day's journeys, a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) can be waiting when you are ready.

## Discretion and your pace

Every appointment respects your privacy, schedule and preferences. The aim is for you to feel at ease and ready, without unnecessary pressure. To set a date, you can [book the Glam Team for your preparation](/en/request/?service=glam-team), mentioning the occasion, the place and the time.
