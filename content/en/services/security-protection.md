## What we arrange

Security covers three distinct areas that often overlap: people, places and information. In each one, we start from your actual situation rather than a standard package.

### Personal protection

- Close protection and discreet accompaniment at public or business appearances
- Risk assessment covering your travel, your residences and your schedule
- Advance review of venues and routes
- Planning of movements and schedules
- Access control and monitoring of entry points
- Emergency procedures prepared and their execution coordinated
- Liaison with police, fire and emergency services when required
- Prevention advice: recommended day-to-day habits and behaviour

### Residential and property security

- Inspection of the residence or premises and identification of vulnerabilities
- Access-control planning (badges, codes, key management)
- Coordination of monitoring, patrols and alarm response
- Oversight of equipment maintenance: cameras, alarms, access control
- Briefing household staff on procedures, including evacuation
- Incident logging and reporting to those concerned
- Regular review of procedures

### Information security

Where useful, we introduce you to independent specialists who assess the risks to your information and digital tools and recommend appropriate measures.

## How a request works

1. **First contact.** Tell us only the type of need, the dates and the place. Please do not include sensitive security details in the website form.
2. **Assessment.** In a confidential conversation, we review the situation with you and, where needed, with the intended partner: context, places, schedule, people concerned.
3. **Proposal.** Specific capabilities, number of personnel, terms and the legal framework are confirmed before engagement.
4. **The assignment.** The partner works to the agreed arrangements; The Valet remains your contact for organisation and adjustments.
5. **Follow-up.** Procedures are reviewed with you when your situation, places or schedule change.

## Typical situations

- A visit of a few days to Geneva with public appointments, for which discreet accompaniment and routes prepared in advance are wanted.
- A private reception where access control and guest management must remain discreet.
- A residence left empty for part of the year, for which monitoring and alarm response need to be organised.
- Moving valuables between two addresses, with accompaniment suited to what is being moved.
- A family stay in a resort, during which the children's movements need careful organisation.

## Where

**Geneva.** Assignments are most frequent here: travel around town, appointments, arrivals at Geneva Airport (GVA), residences. The city's major international gatherings mean partner availability must be checked early.

**Vaud and Valais.** Lakeside residences, estates, chalets in Verbier or Zermatt: residential security and travel support adapt to more isolated settings.

**Gstaad and St-Moritz.** In season, stays often include evenings and public appearances; planning takes the distance from Geneva into account. For accommodation, see our [chalets in Gstaad](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/#gstaad).

**The French Alps.** In Courchevel, Megève or Chamonix, French private-security regulations apply; the partner must be licensed in France.

## Who does what

The Valet takes your request, organises the assessment, selects the partner, coordinates the assignment with your other services (chauffeur, residence, events) and remains your contact.

The Valet does not itself provide armed or close protection. These assignments, and property monitoring, are coordinated through independent, appropriately licensed partners, within the law applicable where they take place. Information-security specialists also work independently.

## Alongside your other services

Security is most effective when it is part of the rest of your arrangements. Routes are prepared with your [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/), access and keys with our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/), and arrangements for a reception with the team planning your [wedding or private event](/en/services/weddings-private-events/).

## The framework and confidentiality

All security services are subject to availability, local law, licensing requirements and a prior assessment. Specific capabilities are confirmed before engagement. What you tell us is shared only with those who need it for the assignment. For a first conversation, you can [request a confidential security consultation](/en/request/?service=security), giving only the type of need, the dates and the place.
