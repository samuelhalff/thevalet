A security request is not handled like a booking. Before talking about officers, vehicles or cameras, the situation has to be understood: who needs protecting, from what, where, for how long, and with what level of visibility. That is why everything here starts with an assessment, and why every service described on this page remains subject to confirmation.

## The framework: who does what

The Valet does not itself provide protection services. We introduce and coordinate independent partners, chosen for their authorised activity, who act under their own responsibility. Private security is regulated by law, and the rules differ from one canton to another as well as between countries: what a partner can do, where and on what terms, is confirmed by that partner before any commitment.

Our role is to take your request, pass it to the right contact, organise the assessment, then coordinate the service with the rest of your programme: travel, residence, events.

## First step: the assessment

The assessment is carried out with a partner. It covers the people concerned, their routines and movements, the places involved (residence, office, event venues), public exposure and any particular concerns. It leads to a written proposal: scope, hours, number of personnel, communication arrangements and terms. It may also conclude that a light arrangement is enough, or that a requested service is not possible within the legal framework.

## Personal protection: services our partners can provide

- close protection and a discreet presence at public or professional outings;
- planning of movements and schedules, together with your [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/);
- advance reconnaissance of places to be visited;
- checking access and entry points at a reception;
- discreet observation of the surroundings;
- incident protocols and liaison with emergency services and authorities where needed;
- prevention advice for the protected person and those around them.

## Protecting residences and property

For a villa, apartment or chalet, partners can provide:

- an inspection of the premises to identify weak points;
- recommendations on, or installation of, alarm, video and access-control systems by specialist companies;
- patrols or a presence during an absence or an event;
- alarm handling and coordination with the relevant authorities;
- equipment maintenance and incident reports;
- briefing household staff on procedures.

If your residence is already looked after by our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) service, we coordinate access, keys and presence between the two.

## Information and digital discretion

Security also depends on what circulates: shared diaries, published photos, connected devices in the home. On request, specialist partners can review these points and propose suitable measures. On our side we apply a simple rule: the less information circulates, the better it is protected. We share with a supplier only what they need to do their work.

## Sending a request

The website form is not meant for describing a sensitive situation. Give only the type of need (person, residence, event), the place, the dates and a way to reach you, then [request a security assessment](/en/request/?service=security). We then agree with you and the partner on a suitable channel for the details.

Lead times depend on the type of arrangement, the place, the number of personnel and the period: some weeks, such as those of major international conferences in Geneva, are in high demand. A request for the next day can be looked at, with no certainty that it can be met.

## Security at events

A wedding, a reception at home or a launch may call for guest checks at the entrance or a discreet presence near certain people. When we organise the event with you through our [weddings and private events](/en/services/weddings-private-events/) service, the arrangement is built into the running order from the start and coordinated with the venue and other suppliers.

If you are in immediate danger, call the police on 117 or 112: The Valet is not an emergency service.
