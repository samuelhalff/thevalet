## What we arrange

Managing a villa in Geneva or an apartment by the lake depends on regular care and on someone who keeps the whole picture in view. We take on that role: we organise the tasks, choose the providers and report back to you.

### Housekeeping

- Cleaning of living areas: vacuuming, washing floors, dusting surfaces
- Preparing bedrooms: changing bed linen, tidying personal belongings
- Cleaning bathrooms and restocking toiletries
- Care of furniture, household appliances and equipment
- Waste, sorting and recycling
- Laundry: washing, ironing and putting away household linen and clothes
- Supplies: cleaning products, towels, paper, everyday essentials
- Inspection and reporting of damage, needed repairs or missing items

### Beyond cleaning

- Managing keys and access for providers and visitors
- Welcoming your guests, deliveries and tradespeople
- Following up repairs and maintenance work
- Care of the garden, terraces and outdoor areas
- Coordinating everyone who works in the house

## How it is set up

1. **A visit or a conversation.** We learn how the house runs: rooms, equipment, habits, what needs doing and what must not be touched.
2. **A proposal.** Tasks, frequency, providers and terms are set out before anything is agreed. You can [request residence management for your home](/en/request/?service=residence-management) to begin.
3. **A house book.** We write the instructions with you: products to use, private rooms, access rules, useful contacts.
4. **The visits.** Providers follow the house book; we schedule and check their work.
5. **Reporting.** You receive a regular summary, and we flag anything that needs a decision.

## Typical situations

- **A second home left empty for months**, to be opened, aired, cleaned and stocked before you arrive, then closed after you leave.
- **A family house with a stream of guests**: rooms to prepare, linen to change, welcomes to arrange.
- **A leak or breakdown while you are away**, needing a tradesperson, a key handover and follow-up until the repair is done.
- **Moving in**: a first deep clean, linen and supplies put in place, deliveries coordinated.
- **A garden to keep through the seasons**, with visits adapted to spring and autumn.
- **A dinner or reception at home**, with the house put back in order the next day.

## Where we work

**Geneva**: villas, houses and apartments in the city and canton, where we coordinate regular or one-off visits.

**The canton of Vaud**: residences by the lake and inland, often occupied for part of the year, which puts more weight on preparation before arrival and on keeping an eye on the house while the owners are away.

**The Alpine resorts**: chalets in Valais, Gstaad, St-Moritz and the French Alps. In the mountains, residence management follows the seasons: opening before winter, upkeep during stays, closing in spring. See chalet management in [Verbier](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/#verbier) or [Gstaad](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/#gstaad).

What changes from place to place: the providers available locally, winter access and how often the house is occupied.

## Who does what

**The Valet coordinates.** We select the providers, schedule their visits, pass on your instructions, hold the keys under the agreed rules, check the work and report back. We are your single contact for the house.

**Providers do the work.** Cleaning, laundry, gardening and repairs are carried out by independent providers and tradespeople chosen for each task. They confirm their terms before starting.

**Specialist services stay separate.** Monitoring, alarms or protection of the house are handled by specialist partners in [security and protection](/en/services/security-protection/), whom we can coordinate separately.

## Preparing the house before you arrive

For a second home, we draw up an arrival list with you: opening and airing, heating, a full clean, beds made, linen and towels in place, the fridge stocked to your preferences, flowers if you wish, equipment checked. If you arrive through Geneva Airport, we also coordinate your journey with a [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/). After you leave, we close the house according to your instructions and tell you what to plan for the next stay.
