## Day-to-day upkeep of the house

Housekeeping is the heart of residence management: what keeps a house clean, tidy and ready to live in without you having to think about it. Depending on the agreed brief, it covers:

1. **Shared areas**: floors vacuumed and washed, surfaces dusted, living rooms put back in order.
2. **Bedrooms and bathrooms**: beds changed, bathrooms cleaned, personal belongings tidied, supplies renewed.
3. **Waste and recycling**: collection, sorting and bags replaced to the commune's schedule.
4. **Furniture and equipment**: furniture, appliances and equipment cared for as they need.
5. **Supplies**: toiletries, towels, paper and household supplies kept available.
6. **Laundry**: household linen and clothes washed, ironed and put away.
7. **Inspection and reporting**: damage, needed repairs or missing items spotted and reported.
8. **Coordination** with the other people working in the house.

## Keys, access, visitors and maintenance

Beyond cleaning, residence management covers what happens around the house. We manage keys and access according to rules agreed with you, welcome visitors, tradespeople and deliveries, and follow repairs and maintenance through to completion. The garden is entrusted to gardeners we coordinate, on a calendar suited to the seasons. For alarms, monitoring or access control, we call on our [security and protection](/en/services/security-protection/) partners: these services are not part of residence management itself.

## Before you arrive, while you are away

Many residences in the region are occupied only part of the year. While you are away, we can arrange regular visits: airing, collecting post, a visual check, watering. Before you return, the house is prepared to your instructions: heating back on, beds made, fridge filled, flowers. We can also time this preparation to your arrival, together with our [private chauffeur in Geneva](/en/services/private-chauffeur-geneva/) for the airport welcome. For a dinner or reception at home, residence management works with our [private events](/en/services/weddings-private-events/) service.

## How we write the brief

Everything starts with a visit or a detailed conversation about the house. We will ask about:

- the size, number of rooms and particular spaces (cellar, pool, outbuildings);
- the rhythm you want: daily, a few visits a week, seasonal;
- the house rules: rooms not to enter, products to avoid, fragile items, pets;
- who gives instructions and who should be kept informed;
- how keys, codes and access are handled today.

The brief is written, given to the people doing the work and reviewed with you when your needs change. Requests received 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; lead times and availability confirmed for each request.

## What is included and what is ordered separately

The scope is set in the proposal. Usually, housekeeping, laundry, everyday supplies, key management and supervision of the people involved are included. Repairs, technical maintenance (heating, pool, installations), major garden work and large purchases are ordered separately, on a quote and with your agreement. Supplies bought for you appear on a statement. For anything beyond the house, such as reservations, gifts or travel, our [concierge in Geneva](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/) takes over.

## The people involved and how they are supervised

The Valet selects the people who do the work (household staff, cleaning companies, gardeners, tradespeople), gives them the brief and follows their work. Technical work is carried out by independent companies, which issue their own quotes. We confirm in writing who works in your home, how often and on what terms, and let you know of any change.

Follow-up runs at the pace that suits you: a short report after each visit, a weekly update or a simple message when something needs your decision. Comments from the people who live in the house, residents, guests or staff, are gathered and passed on to those concerned. For a mountain home, the same organisation applies to your chalet; our pages on [chalets and residences in the Alps](/en/services/alpine-chalets-residences/) explain what we arrange during a resort stay.

To start, you can [describe your residence and what you need](/en/request/?service=residence-management): we come back to you to arrange a visit or a first conversation.
