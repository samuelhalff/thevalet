## What we arrange

A good mountain stay depends as much on what surrounds the chalet as on the chalet itself: the arrival, a house ready for you, meals, skiing, children, evenings. We find the accommodation and coordinate the rest.

### Accommodation

- **Private chalets** to rent by the week, for a long weekend or for the season
- **Private residences and apartments** in the heart of the villages or further out
- **Mountain hotels**, when hotel service suits your stay better
- Selection to your criteria: number of bedrooms, access to the slopes, quiet, wellness area, rooms for children, winter access

### Around the stay

- Transfers from Geneva and between resorts; see our [Geneva–Alps transfers](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/)
- Chalet housekeeping: cleaning, linen, groceries before arrival, tidying on departure
- A private chef for dinners or for the whole week
- Ski passes, equipment rental with your sizes, lessons with local schools or instructors
- Restaurant bookings, treatments, activities and experiences
- Hair and makeup for an evening with our [Glam Team at home](/en/services/glam-team-at-home/)

## How a booking works

1. **Your request.** Resort or region, dates, number of guests and bedrooms, indicative budget, services wanted. You can [request a chalet for your stay](/en/request/?service=chalets) even if the resort is not yet decided.
2. **Our selection.** We contact owners, agencies and hotels, check availability and present a short selection with each one's terms.
3. **The booking.** You choose; the contract is made with the owner, agency or hotel, on their terms. We follow the booking and scheduled payments.
4. **Preparation.** Transfers, groceries, chef, passes, equipment, ski lessons: we arrange the services you choose before you arrive.
5. **During the stay.** You keep a single contact for every request, from a dinner table to a change in the return transfer.

## Typical situations

- **A family arriving at the end of the day** with young children: chalet heated, beds made, a meal ready, ski equipment already booked.
- **A group of friends for a ski week**, with a chef for dinners and very different skiing levels.
- **A summer stay** around a festival, with tickets to book and hikes to organise.
- **A reception or birthday in a chalet**, with catering, decoration and guests to transport.
- **A stay in two resorts**, for example Verbier then Zermatt, with a transfer between them.
- **A whole season** in a chalet, with regular housekeeping and a stream of guests.

## Where we arrange stays

Each resort has its own character, access and calendar. Here is what matters when choosing and preparing a stay.

### Verbier

Verbier overlooks the Val de Bagnes in Valais and opens onto the 4 Vallées ski area. The resort is known for off-piste skiing and freeride, showcased at the end of each winter by the Xtreme Verbier; in summer, the Verbier Festival makes it a classical music destination. Chalets are spread between the village centre and quieter areas, closer to or further from the lifts. In Verbier we coordinate chalet concierge, housekeeping, a private chef, passes and equipment. For the arrival, see the [transfer to Verbier](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#verbier).

### Zermatt

Zermatt, at the foot of the Matterhorn, is car-free: people get around on foot or in electric vehicles, which gives the village its calm. The ski area connects with Italy and allows glacier skiing in summer. In spring, the Zermatt Unplugged festival livens up the resort. The absence of cars makes arrival more involved: we coordinate the change at Täsch, luggage transport and the welcome at the chalet. See the [transfer to Zermatt](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#zermatt).

### Gstaad

Gstaad, in the Saanenland (Bernese Oberland), has kept its wooden chalet architecture and village atmosphere. The winter season is followed by a full summer, set to the rhythm of the Menuhin Festival Gstaad. Many stay as families or for private receptions. We coordinate housekeeping, private chefs, bookings and event organisation there; for a party or wedding, see our [weddings and private events](/en/services/weddings-private-events/). For the arrival, see the [transfer to Gstaad](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#gstaad).

### St-Moritz

St-Moritz lies in the Upper Engadine, in Graubünden, beside a lake that freezes in winter. The lake hosts the Snow Polo World Cup St. Moritz and the White Turf races. The resort is far from Geneva: many travellers arrive via Zurich or through Samedan (Engadin Airport). In St-Moritz we coordinate accommodation, bookings for these events subject to availability, concierge support and transport. See the [transfer to St-Moritz](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#st-moritz).

### Courchevel

Courchevel, in Savoie, is part of Les 3 Vallées ski area. The resort is spread over several villages at different altitudes, each with its own atmosphere, from lively to family-oriented. An altiport takes small aircraft and helicopters. We search for chalets and hotels there and coordinate private chefs, housekeeping, passes and bookings. See the [transfer to Courchevel](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#courchevel).

### Megève

Megève, in Haute-Savoie, has kept an old village centre with pedestrian streets and horse-drawn carriages in winter, facing the Mont Blanc massif. The resort is enjoyed as much in summer as in winter, for golf, hiking and dining. It has an altiport. We coordinate chalets, housekeeping, private chefs, activities and bookings there. See the [transfer to Megève](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#megeve).

### Chamonix

Chamonix, at the foot of Mont Blanc, is above all a mountain town: mountaineering, off-piste skiing, the Aiguille du Midi cable car. The Mont Blanc Tunnel links the valley to Italy. Every late summer, the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc (UTMB) fills the valley, and accommodation is booked early. We coordinate accommodation, concierge support, housekeeping and transfers there, and can arrange outings with independent high-mountain guides. See the [transfer to Chamonix](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#chamonix).

## Who does what

**The Valet coordinates.** We search for properties, compare options, present the terms to you, follow the booking and arrange the services for your stay. We remain your single contact before, during and after.

**Landlords let.** The chalet, residence or room is let by its owner, an agency or a hotel, on their terms. Cancellation, deposit and arrival rules are theirs; we set them out clearly before anything is agreed.

**Providers deliver.** Chefs, housekeepers, ski schools, instructors and guides are independent professionals chosen locally. Transfers are provided by independent partners appropriately licensed for passenger transport.

## Private chef, housekeeping and experiences

A private chef can prepare breakfasts, dinners or a single evening, to your tastes and everyone's dietary needs. Chalet housekeeping follows the same approach as our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/): cleaning, linen and supplies, at the pace of your stay. For experiences, we look for what suits your group: a day with a guide, a table at altitude, a treatment after skiing, a sleigh ride, an evening in the village.

## Chalet, residence or hotel: how to choose

A private chalet offers the space and privacy of a home, with services arranged around it. A residence or apartment suits shorter stays or small groups who want to be in the heart of the village. A hotel brings service on site, useful for a short stay or when you would rather manage nothing. We help you decide according to the size of the group, the length of stay, the season and the level of service you expect, then book the option you choose.
