## Chalet, residence or hotel: finding the right place

The choice of accommodation shapes the whole stay. A chalet offers space, privacy and the freedom to live at your own pace, with a chef and housekeeping organised around you. A private residence, apartment or house, suits longer stays or families who want a base without daily services. A hotel is often the simplest option for a few nights, for a couple or when you want a restaurant and spa on site.

We start from your plans, not from a catalogue: the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, how close you want to be to the slopes or the village, the children's needs, space to work, a sauna or pool, step-free access for an older relative. We contact owners and agencies, check availability, then present a few compared options, with what is included and the cancellation terms.

## What we prepare for the stay

Once the accommodation is booked, the chalet concierge takes over:

- **before arrival**: chalet prepared, fridge filled from your list, cots set up, arrival times passed to the owner;
- **a private chef** for a few dinners or the whole stay, with menus agreed in advance;
- **chalet housekeeping** during the stay, laundry and cleaning, together with our [residence management](/en/services/residence-management/) service;
- **ski passes, equipment hire and lessons** booked with the resort's schools and rental shops;
- **restaurants, activities and mountain guides** for outings off the slopes;
- **hair and make-up** for an evening, with our [Glam Team at home](/en/services/glam-team-at-home/);
- **dinners and parties** at the chalet, coordinated with our [event planning](/en/services/weddings-private-events/) service.

The journey from Geneva is covered by our [private transfers to the Alps](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/), which set out how to reach each resort.

## Choosing your resort

Each resort has its own character, season and calendar. Here is what sets them apart for a stay.

### Verbier

Verbier is part of the 4 Vallées ski area. The resort is busy in winter and in summer, with hiking and mountain biking in the warmer months, and its accommodation is mostly chalets and apartments around a lively centre. Every summer, the Verbier Festival brings classical musicians together; at the end of winter, Xtreme Verbier is held on the Bec des Rosses. We arrange chalet, private chef and ski passes; for the journey, see the [transfer from Geneva to Verbier](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#verbier).

### Zermatt

Zermatt lies at the foot of the Matterhorn. Its ski area links with Breuil-Cervinia in Italy, and the resort is open in winter and summer, with high-altitude skiing and mountaineering. In spring, the Zermatt Unplugged festival stages concerts. For a stay in Zermatt, we coordinate arrival at the accommodation, the chef and guides; for access, see the [transfer from Geneva to Zermatt](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#zermatt).

### Gstaad

Gstaad's village centre is built around a largely pedestrian promenade. The resort is known for its chalets, quiet atmosphere and two seasons. Every summer, the Menuhin Festival Gstaad hosts classical concerts and the Swiss Open Gstaad a tennis tournament. Gstaad suits family stays as well as [weddings and private events in the mountains](/en/services/weddings-private-events/); for the road, see the [transfer from Geneva to Gstaad](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#gstaad).

### St-Moritz

St-Moritz has hosted the Winter Olympics twice, in 1928 and 1948. Winter is partly lived on the frozen lake, home to the Snow Polo World Cup St. Moritz and the White Turf horse races; the Cresta Run and the natural-ice bobsleigh run are part of the resort's tradition. For a stay during these events, book early; for the journey, see how to [reach St-Moritz from Geneva](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#st-moritz).

### Courchevel

Courchevel is part of the Trois Vallées ski area. The resort brings together several villages at different levels, including Courchevel 1850, Moriond and Le Praz, and is mainly a winter resort, with chalets, apartments and hotels. Choosing the village matters as much as choosing the chalet: we help you decide according to your group and skiing level. For access, see the [transfer from Geneva to Courchevel](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#courchevel).

### Megève

Megève is a historic village resort with a pedestrian medieval centre. It belongs to the Évasion Mont-Blanc ski area and is open in winter and summer. Its chalets suit family stays and gatherings of friends; we arrange private chefs, housekeeping and activities there. For the journey, see the [transfer from Geneva to Megève](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#megeve).

### Chamonix

Chamonix hosted the first Winter Olympics, in 1924, and remains a centre of mountaineering, winter and summer. The Aiguille du Midi cable car and the Montenvers train to the Mer de Glace leave from the valley; the Vallée Blanche is an off-piste descent, done with a mountain guide. Every late summer, the UTMB circles Mont Blanc. We book guides, accommodation and activities; for access, see the [transfer from Geneva to Chamonix](/en/services/geneva-alps-transfers/#chamonix).

## Calendar: seasons, busy weeks and booking

Requests received 24 hours a day, 7 days a week; lead times and availability confirmed for each request. In winter, the most requested weeks are Christmas and New Year and the Swiss and French school holidays; many chalets are then let by the week, Saturday to Saturday. Major resort events also fill the accommodation. Outside these periods, lengths of stay are more flexible and the choice wider. In summer, the season is shorter and some properties open for only part of the year: we check opening dates before proposing an option.

## Your stay request, point by point

To propose suitable chalets, we will ask for:

1. **The resort, or the two or three you are considering**, and your dates, with any flexibility.
2. **The group**: number of adults and children, ages, bedrooms and bathrooms needed.
3. **Your priorities**: ski-in ski-out, close to the village, quiet, wellness area, a study.
4. **The services you want**: chef, daily housekeeping, ski passes, lessons, equipment, activities for children.
5. **The budget** for the accommodation, and what you want included or not.
6. **Constraints**: pets, accessibility, food allergies to pass on to the chef.

## Departure day and afterwards

The end of a chalet stay also takes some organising. Contracts often include an inventory check, a departure time and a deposit, whose terms are given to you when booking. We agree the key handover time with the owner or agency, have hired equipment returned and settle the stay's final bills with the providers, presented on a statement. If something has been left behind, we arrange for it to be sent on. And if you liked the chalet, we can ask the owner about availability for the following season, before the busiest weeks are taken.

## Owners, agencies and providers: who does what

The chalet belongs to an owner and is let directly or through an agency, under a contract whose terms are given to you before booking. Services during the stay are delivered by local providers: chefs, housekeeping companies, ski schools, rental shops, guides. The Valet searches, compares, books and coordinates; we confirm in writing who is involved, what is included and on what terms. For daily life in Geneva between stays, see our [concierge in Geneva](/en/services/private-concierge-geneva/).
